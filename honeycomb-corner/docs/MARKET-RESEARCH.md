# Market research: gem store pricing for Honeycomb Corner

Research date: 30 September 2026. Companion to `GEM-STORE.md`.

## How to read this report

- **Verified (search extract)**: the figure appeared in a web search result
  summary pointing at the cited URL. In this session the fetch tool was blocked
  for every store and wiki domain tried (apps.apple.com, play.google.com,
  fandom.com, helpshift.com, psprices.com), so **no page was opened directly**.
  Treat these figures as "reported by the source", and re-check them in the
  App Store / Play Store listing before relying on them. Store prices also
  change over time and by region.
- **Estimate / derived**: my own arithmetic or judgement from the verified
  figures. Always labelled.
- **Not found**: said explicitly.

## 2. Starter packs and one-time packs

- Idle Miner Tycoon: "Starter Package 2" $4.99; one-off permanent boosts at
  $4.99 to $6.99 (Permanent 2x Idle Cash $4.99; 2x Cash Boost + Idle Cash
  $6.99); No Ads Bundle $19.99 including 1,200 Super Cash. [IMT wiki](https://idleminertycoon.fandom.com/wiki/The_Shop), [IMT Super Cash](https://idleminertycoon.fandom.com/wiki/Super_Cash)
- Other idle-game starter packs: Idle Brick Breaker $2.49; DPS IDLE $2.99;
  Idle Champions starter pack seen as low as $1.49. [Idle Brick Breaker wiki](https://idle-brick-breaker.fandom.com/wiki/Store), [GG.deals DPS IDLE](https://gg.deals/dlc/dps-idle-starter-pack/#recommended), [cost.games](https://cost.games/us/games/idle_champions_starter_pack/)
- Industry view: Deconstructor of Fun argues starter packs are often priced
  too low in the chase for conversion (a $2.99 offer goes to players who would
  have bought a $19.99+ pack), and that the first price a player pays anchors
  later spending; they recommend testing rather than defaulting to a
  "no-brainer" price. Survey figures in the article: 85.2% of developers
  design starter packs for fast conversion, 61.1% price below willingness to
  pay. [Deconstructor of Fun, Apr 2024](https://www.deconstructoroffun.com/blog/2024/4/8/free-to-play-starter-pack-pricing-when-conversion-is-king-we-may-price-too-low)
- Supporter packs in cozy games are mostly a PC/Steam practice (e.g. Sugardew
  Island, Garden Life); **prices not found** in search. [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3494110/Sugardew_Island__Your_cozy_farm_shop__Supporter_Pack/), [IsThereAnyDeal](https://isthereanydeal.com/game/garden-life-a-cozy-simulator-supporter-pack/info/)
- **Extra builder benchmark (closest analogue to the Supporter pack):** in
  Clash of Clans the 2nd builder hut costs 250 gems (3rd 500, 4th 1,000, 5th
  2,000). [CoC wiki: Builder's Hut](https://clashofclans.fandom.com/wiki/Builder's_Hut).
  At ~100 gems per $ that is roughly **$2.50** (estimate). The Builder's
  Apprentice (a time helper) costs 500 gems, reported as "over 3 USD".
  [Sportskeeda](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/builder-s-apprentice-clash-clans-cost-tips-use)

## 3. How timer-skip currency is priced

- Clash of Clans' published skip formula: under a day, gems = 20 + 11 × (hours
  − 1); over a day, gems = 260 + 123 × (days − 1). Short skips cost relatively
  more per hour; the last hour/day is the priciest.
  [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/clash-of-clans-time-monetization-formulas-demistifyed)
- **Derived:** at ~100 gems per $, a 1-hour skip ≈ $0.20, a 1-day skip ≈
  $2.60, a 7-day skip ≈ $10. Skipping a day costs about the same as the
  cheapest paid bundles; that is the "impulse zone".
- Hay Day uses diamonds for nearly every timer (crops, production, buildings,
  boats) [Lootbar guide](https://www.lootbar.com/blog/en/hay-day-diamonds-guide-best-ways-to-use-premium-currency-wisely.html);
  **formula not found**.
- Idle Bee Factory Tycoon uses green cash for time boosters; per-minute cost
  **not found**. [Skich](https://skich.app/games/bee-factory)

## 4. What players praise and complain about

**Complaints**
- Idle Bee Factory Tycoon: forced / unskippable ads, 3 to 4 back-to-back 30s
  ads to progress, ads after every rebirth, **no one-off "no ads" purchase,
  only a $7.99/month VIP** (one review: "$384 a year"), zone waits of an hour
  up to three days. [AppGrooves](https://appgrooves.com/app/bee-factory-by-green-panda-games/negative)
- Idle Miner Tycoon: a minute of closing pop-ups on launch; "something pops up
  every 5 minutes". [JustUseApp reviews](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1116645064/idle-miner-tycoon-gold-rush/reviews)
- Cozy genre: energy timers, pop-ups and constant purchase pushes "break the
  calm" [Aidorable](https://www.aidorable.ai/blog/cozy-mobile-games); live-service
  mechanics in cozy games criticised [ResetEra](https://www.resetera.com/threads/joshgaminggarden-the-problematic-rise-of-live-service-%E2%80%9Ccozy%E2%80%9D-games.1487800/).
- Any monetisation change causes a review spike within 24 to 72 hours; free
  players who hit walls leave and write bad reviews. [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/blog/monetization-paywall-complaints-mobile-game-reviews), [AppFollow guide](https://appfollow.io/blog/what-mobile-game-players-want-monetization-insights-from-app-store-reviews)

**Praise**
- Idle Bee Factory Tycoon: fun, relaxing, 100+ bees to collect. [AppGrooves](https://appgrooves.com/app/bee-factory-by-green-panda-games/negative)
- Idle Miner Tycoon: progress is fair without paying if you check in daily;
  after the no-ads purchase "absolutely zero ads". [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/1116645064/idle-miner-tycoon-gold-rush/reviews)
- Players favour cosmetic-only purchases, one-time purchases over
  subscriptions, and everything earnable through play; Cats & Soup is cited
  for optional, never-forced ads. [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/blog/what-mobile-game-players-want-monetization-insights-from-app-store-reviews), [Choost Games](https://choostgames.com/blog/cozy-mobile-games-no-ads/)

## 5. Practical context

- Apple and Google both charge 15% (not 30%) on the first $1M/year for
  enrolled small developers. [Apple](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/small-business-program/), [Appbot](https://appbot.co/blog/app-developers-apple-google-small-business-programs/)
- Apple offers 900 price points from $0.29 since 2023, so prices need not be
  x.99, though the market convention is. [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/news/?id=dbrszv62)

## 6. Recommended draft price table (estimate, for playtesting)

All prices are **my recommendations** built on the benchmarks above, not
market data. Gem amounts follow the Codigames/Supercell ladder so the
per-dollar curve is familiar; rescale all amounts together once playtests show
what a typical mid-game timer costs in gems.

| Item | Price (USD) | Contents | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handful of gems | $0.99 | 80 gems | Market-standard entry tier (Codigames 80/$0.99) |
| Pouch of gems | $4.99 | 500 gems (+25% value) | Codigames 500/$4.99 |
| Jar of gems | $9.99 | 1,200 gems (+50%) | Codigames 1,200/$9.99 |
| Hive of gems | $19.99 | 2,500 gems (+56%) | Codigames 2,500/$19.99; stop here for a small cozy game (no $49.99/$99.99 tier at launch) |
| Starter pack (once, after first gem-starved moment) | $1.99 (test $2.99) | ~300 gems + 1 cosmetic | Below the $2.49 to $4.99 range seen; roughly 1.5x the value of the $4.99 pack per dollar. A/B test per Deconstructor of Fun |
| Supporter pack (once) | $4.99 (test $6.99) | Second builder + cosmetic set (+ optionally 300 gems) | CoC's 2nd builder ≈ $2.50 of gems; IMT permanent one-offs sit at $4.99 to $6.99; "supporter" framing plus cosmetics justifies the upper end |

**Skip cost calibration (estimate):** aim for skipping a typical mid-game
timer to cost ~10 to 30 gems (roughly $0.10 to $0.30, similar to CoC's
1-hour rate), so a $0.99 bundle feels useful and earned gems still matter.
Use `gemsSpentOnSkips` and `gemStarvedOnGoals` to adjust.

## 7. Do's and don'ts

**Do**
1. Use the familiar $0.99 / $4.99 / $9.99 / $19.99 ladder with rising value.
2. Keep the Supporter pack a **one-time** purchase. Bee Factory's $7.99/month
   VIP with no one-off option is its most-cited complaint.
3. Keep every gem earnable through play (already planned); free players must
   never hit a wall.
4. Show the starter pack once, at a meaningful moment, and test its price
   rather than defaulting to the cheapest tier.
5. Put cosmetics in paid packs; players accept them most readily.
6. Enrol in both stores' 15% small-business programmes.

**Don't**
1. Don't use pop-up offers on launch or during play (Idle Miner complaints).
2. Don't make waits of hours to days a progression gate (Bee Factory's
   "up to three days" complaint).
3. Don't add forced or chained ads; if ads ever appear, make them opt-in only.
4. Don't use subscriptions or $49.99+ packs at launch.
5. Don't change prices or economy abruptly after launch without expecting a
   review spike (AppFollow: 24 to 72 hours).
